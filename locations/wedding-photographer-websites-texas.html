The State

Texas has five distinct wedding markets.

Texas couples don't search "Texas wedding photographer." They search for their city, their venue, their style. Austin has a boho, Hill Country creative energy. San Antonio has the River Walk and the historic missions. Dallas is polished and high-spend. Fort Worth carries its western character. Houston is the largest and most diverse wedding market in the state — and one of the most competitive in the country. Each city has different searches, different competitors, and different expectations. Each needs its own strategy.
