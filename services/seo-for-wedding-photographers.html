What SEO actually means for a photographer.

01

Local intent

Couples search by city, by venue, by style. "Wedding photographer Austin." "[Venue] wedding photographer." Your job is to be the answer. That takes location and service pages built for real phrases.

02

Clean technical structure

Schema, sitemap, meta, semantic HTML, image optimisation. The infrastructure that tells Google exactly what your site is, who it's for, and where you work — before a single piece of content lands.

03

Content that keeps arriving

A one-time setup is a starting point, not a strategy. Ranking requires fresh content and ongoing attention — not a redesign every year, but one good article a month and consistent monitoring.
